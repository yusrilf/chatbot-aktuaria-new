## ✅ **Validation Rules**

### **Withdrawal Benefits Validation:**

- **Rule**: Withdrawal benefits must follow company policy progression
- **Test**: If service_years ≥ 3 AND withdrawal_factor = 0.0
- **Action**: Raise warning "Company policy provides withdrawal benefits from year 3"

### **Maximum Benefit Factor Validation:**

- **Rule**: All factors cap appropriately based on service years
- **Test**: Pension factor caps at 25.75 for 24+ years service
- **Action**: If factor exceeds maximum, apply cap and log adjustment

### **Progressive Enhancement Validation:**

- **Rule**: Withdrawal factors must increase progressively
- **Test**: Later service years should have ≥ earlier withdrawal factors
- **Action**: If regression detected, raise error "Withdrawal factors must be progressive"

### **Company Policy Consistency:**

- **Rule**: All benefit types must align with company policy structure
- **Test**: Verify factor relationships match expected patterns
- **Action**: If inconsistency found, flag for policy review
