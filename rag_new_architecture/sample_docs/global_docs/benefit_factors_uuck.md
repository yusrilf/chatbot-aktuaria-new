# 📊 UUCK Benefit Factor Table

## 🎯 **Regulation Overview**

**Source:** Undang-Undang Cipta Kerja (Omnibus Law)  
**Status:** Current active regulation for new employees
**Coverage:** All new employment contracts since UUCK effective date
**Key Change:** No withdrawal benefits for voluntary resignation

## 🧮 **UUCK Formula Framework**

### **Key Differences from UUK13**

- **No Enhancement Factor**: Direct multiplier application (no 115% boost)
- **Lower Pension Factors**: Reduced retirement benefits
- **No Withdrawal Benefits**: Zero compensation for voluntary resignation
- **Compensation Pay Only**: Limited to mandatory compensation payments

### **Benefit Type Formulas**

| Benefit Type   | Formula                           | Notes                                  |
| -------------- | --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Pension**    | `(1.75B + 1C) + Compensation_Pay` | Reduced multiplier                     |
| **Death**      | `(2B + 1C) + Compensation_Pay`    | Same death protection                  |
| **Disability** | `(2B + 2C) + Compensation_Pay`    | Enhanced disability support            |
| **Withdrawal** | `Compensation_Pay`                | **Zero voluntary resignation benefit** |

### **Key Changes Summary**

- **Pension Benefits**: ~20-22% reduction across all service levels
- **Death Benefits**: ~13% reduction (less severe than pension)
- **Disability Benefits**: ~36% reduction (most severely impacted)
- **Withdrawal Benefits**: **Complete elimination** (100% reduction)

## 📋 **Compensation Pay Calculations**

### **Mandatory Compensation Components**

UUCK still requires minimal compensation payments regardless of resignation reason:

**💫 Formula: UUCK Compensation Pay**
 ```
compensation_pay = (service_months / 12) × total_gaji_saat_valuasi
```
 
**Komponen:**
- service_months: Total months of service
- total_gaji_saat_valuasi: Current gross monthly salary
- Maximum: Limited by regulation caps

## ✅ **Validation Rules**

### **Withdrawal Benefits Validation:**

- **Rule**: All withdrawal benefits must be zero under UUCK
- **Test**: If benefit_type = 'withdrawal' AND factor ≠ 0.0
- **Action**: Raise error "UUCK withdrawal benefits must be 0.0"

### **Maximum Pension Factor Validation:**

- **Rule**: Pension factor caps at 25.75 for 24+ years service
- **Test**: If benefit_type = 'pension' AND service_years ≥ 24 AND factor ≠ 25.75
- **Action**: Raise error "UUCK pension factor caps at 25.75 for 24+ years"

### **Death vs Pension Relationship:**

- **Rule**: Death factor must be ≥ pension factor
- **Test**: If benefit_type = 'death' AND factor < pension_factor
- **Action**: Raise error "UUCK death factor must be ≥ pension factor"

### **Disability Factor Validation:**

- **Rule**: Disability factor must equal death factor under UUCK
- **Test**: Get expected_death_factor for same service_years
- **Action**: If disability_factor ≠ expected_death_factor, raise error

## 🏛️ **Implementation Notes**

### **Transition Rules**

- Existing employees: Continue under UUK13 (grandfathered)
- New employees (post-UUCK): Must use UUCK benefit structure
- Contract amendments: May trigger move to UUCK structure

### **Company Policy Considerations**

- Companies may provide **higher** benefits than UUCK minimums
- Cannot provide **lower** benefits than UUCK requirements
- Additional voluntary benefits (e.g., insurance) remain company discretion

### **Regulatory Compliance**

- Regular updates required as regulations evolve
- Documentation must show compliance with current UUCK provisions
- Actuarial assumptions should reflect UUCK benefit reductions
